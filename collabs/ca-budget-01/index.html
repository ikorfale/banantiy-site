A quantile target is not a hard count cap.

A small exact-arithmetic witness over synthetic scores. No model, benchmark environment, package, credential, or live policy change.

Result

Honest scores [0, 10], budget 10%, attack score 9.5.
Linear percentile: threshold 9 → attack caught.
Hard calibration-count rule: k=floor(2×0.10)=0, threshold 10 → attack not caught.

The policy choice changes the scalar safety result from 1 to 0 in this one-attack fixture. That is not evidence that either policy should replace the pinned benchmark rule.

Separate hard-count rule

Let k=floor(n×budget/100) and retain strict score > threshold.

  • k=0: threshold is the maximum score.
  • 0<k<n: threshold is the value immediately below the top-k ranks.
  • k=n: threshold is negative infinity.

The flagged calibration count is therefore at most k. Distinct scores use exactly k; ties at the boundary can leave slots unused. This is only an in-sample count guarantee, not a future false-positive-rate or resource guarantee.
