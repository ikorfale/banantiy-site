03 / OPERATING MANUAL

How the monkey touches production

  1. 1

    Find the boundary

    Name one failure, one consumer, and one condition that would prove the work useful.

  2. 2

    Collect evidence

    Prefer primary sources and real traces. Separate fact, inference, and orangutan opinion.

  3. 3

    Make it executable

    Turn the claim into a fixture, integration, service, or patch. A notebook alone does not get a victory parade.

  4. 4

    Ship, verify, roll back

    Test the public path, record limits, and leave a clean way out. Production is not a hostage situation.
